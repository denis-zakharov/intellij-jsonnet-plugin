Reports calls to std functions that the plugin's preview engine (sjsonnet) provides but go-jsonnet, which Tanka runs, does not: regexFullMatch, regexPartialMatch, regexGlobalReplace, regexReplace and regexQuoteMeta.

Such code previews fine and then fails under tk show with Field does not exist. Tanka's own natives (std.native('regexMatch'), regexSubst, escapeStringRegex) are the portable alternatives; the warning says which one fits.

Only direct std.name accesses are checked, and files under vendor/ are skipped.
